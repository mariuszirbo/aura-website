Cookies

Cookie notice

17 November 2026 · The Romanian text prevails

auralab.ro sets no cookies. It does not use advertising cookies, analytics cookies, or local storage to remember a language. Romanian and English are ordinary pages. If the two texts of this notice differ, the Romanian text at auralab.ro/cookie-uri prevails.

Because this website does not set non-essential cookies, it does not show a consent banner. A banner would not add a choice the site needs.

What a visit does

Opening a page downloads the page, its images, its styles and its fonts from auralab.ro. The fonts are files hosted with the site. The request does not go to a third-party font service. The site does not embed advertising, social pixels or an analytics script.

Your browser may keep a copy of those files in its own cache. That is the browser’s copy, not a cookie set by the operator. You can clear it in the browser.

If the hosting provider keeps a short connection log, that log is described in the Privacy notice. A server log is not a cookie placed in the browser.

Sign in is a different site

The Sign in link leaves auralab.ro and opens the A.U.R.A. application. That application may set cookies that it needs in order to keep a session. Those cookies are not set by this marketing website, and they are governed by the application and by the order, not by a banner on auralab.ro.

Contact

Questions about this notice go to sales@auralab.ro, or through the contact form on the English homepage. The commercial rules are in the Terms.
