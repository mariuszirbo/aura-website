AURAAI

Assists the laboratory. Never replaces it.

What AuraAI does

  • Explains the application and the statistics used in a study.
  • Consults the laboratory APS library before a specification is proposed.
  • Runs illustrative calculations. These are not the official study result.
  • Drafts a policy only after the user confirms the content.

What AuraAI does not do

  • Does not approve or withdraw a policy or a study.
  • Does not replace the official statistics produced by the application.
  • Does not invent performance specifications absent from the APS library.
  • Does not interpret patient results or offer a clinical opinion.
