What A.U.R.A. is, and what it is not

A.U.R.A. is business software for analytical performance management. It is intended to help a medical laboratory keep method definitions, acceptance criteria, verification studies, an audit trail and audit-ready outputs in one controlled workspace, for examination processes under ISO 15189:2022.

A.U.R.A. is not a laboratory information system. It is not offered as a medical device or as an in vitro diagnostic. It does not diagnose, treat or monitor a patient, and it does not interpret patient results. It does not accredit a laboratory and it is not a substitute for a qualified person, for the laboratory’s own validation, or for the judgement of an assessor.

References to CLSI protocols, ISO standards, EFLM biological variation or any other external source describe the material the product is built against. They are not a certification, an endorsement, or a statement that every option in that document is implemented. CLSI, ISO, EFLM, RENAR and the names of analyser manufacturers remain the property of their owners. A.U.R.A. is not affiliated with them.

Words on the homepage, in a presentation or in a demonstration describe the intended product. They are not a warranty. They do not promise that an auditor or an accreditation body will accept a record, that a calculation will be free of error, or that the laboratory will meet ISO 15189.
