This notice explains what auralab.ro does with personal data. It is written so that a visitor, a laboratory and a partner can read it without guessing. The Romanian text at auralab.ro/confidentialitate prevails if the two texts differ.

The controller for this website is the legal entity that operates auralab.ro, named in the order or licence agreement. Its name, registration number and registered office will appear there and, once available, on this page. Refer privacy requests to sales@auralab.ro, or use the contact form on auralab.ro/en. This website and the application are separate

This notice covers the marketing website auralab.ro, including the English pages under /en/. Sign in opens the A.U.R.A. application, which is a different service. Accounts, seats and any laboratory data are handled there, under the order and, where personal data are processed for the organisation, under the processor terms in the Terms.

On this website the operator is the controller of the little personal data the site actually causes to exist. Inside the application, the organisation is the controller of personal data in its methods, studies and other records, and the operator is the processor. That split is deliberate. The operator does not take on the laboratory’s duty as controller of patient or staff data that the laboratory chooses to upload.

What this website collects

The contact form, the licence estimate and the technical-presentation request send the details you enter to sales@auralab.ro. The website passes that message to Resend (resend.com) for delivery and does not keep its own copy. A short confirmation is also sent to the address entered, from noreply@auralab.ro. If delivery does not complete, the same text opens as a draft in your own email application. The message contains the personal data you put in it. For a contact or presentation request that is whether you are a laboratory or a reseller, plus your name, organisation, work email, role and message. For a licence estimate it is the months, users, reports and currency you enter, plus your name, organisation, work email and message. The page does not show a price. Do not put patient identifiers, patient results or other health data in it. If you do, the operator may delete the message.

The website sets no cookies and does not use advertising or analytics tools. It does not store a language preference. Romanian and English are separate pages. Fonts are files served by auralab.ro itself, so loading a page does not send your IP address to a font provider.

The site is static. If the hosting provider keeps ordinary connection logs, such as IP address, user agent, time and the address requested, those logs are used only to keep the site secure and available, for a short period. The legal basis is legitimate interest in operating and protecting the site. The host acts on the operator’s instructions or, for its own security logs, as an independent controller of that narrow record.

If you follow Sign in, you leave this website. The application you open may collect account data and whatever the organisation then submits. That collection is not done by this page.

Why, and on what basis

Correspondence that you actually send is used to answer the request, to prepare a demonstration or a commercial proposal, and to keep a record of that business conversation. The legal basis is steps toward a contract with the organisation you name, and the operator’s legitimate interest in answering professional enquiries. The operator does not use these messages for advertising lists and does not sell personal data.

Connection logs, if the host keeps them, are used for security and availability. The legal basis is legitimate interest. They are not used to profile visitors.

Where the application processes personal data for a laboratory, the basis and the instructions are those in the order and in the Terms. This marketing notice does not enlarge them.

Who receives personal data

The operator does not sell personal data and does not share it with advertisers. A message sent from a form on this website is delivered by Resend to sales@auralab.ro and is then handled by the email service the operator uses for that mailbox. A message you send from your own email application is received the same way. Connection logs are handled by the hosting provider.

The operator discloses personal data if the law or a competent authority requires it, and to professional advisers who are bound to confidentiality, where that is needed to establish or defend a legal claim.

Subprocessors of the application, if any, are named in the order or by a notice in the application. For the forms on this website, Resend delivers the message. Resend is outside the European Economic Area. That transfer is made with a tool required by Chapter V of the GDPR.

How long it is kept

The website does not keep its own copy of a form. A message that is delivered is kept in the operator’s mailbox for the commercial conversation and then for as long as the statutory limitation period and any accounting duty require, and no longer for this purpose. If delivery fails and you do not send the draft, the website has nothing to keep. Security logs are kept for a short period set by the host’s ordinary operation.

Customer Data in the application is kept for the licence term and for the 30-day export window described in the Terms, unless the law requires a longer or a shorter period. The organisation can ask for an export in the formats the application already provides.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and Romanian Law 190/2018, a person whose data the operator holds as controller may ask to access it, to correct it, to erase it, to restrict it, to receive a portable copy where the law provides one, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. Where processing is based on consent, consent can be withdrawn. These rights do not erase a record the operator must keep by law, and the operator may ask for enough information to confirm that the request comes from the right person or from the organisation that controls the data.

A request is made to sales@auralab.ro, through the contact form, or through the email address in the order once that address is in force. The operator answers within one month, or within the longer period the GDPR allows for a complex request, and will say if it extends the time.

You may lodge a complaint with the Romanian supervisory authority, the Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal, at dataprotection.ro. That right does not remove the others.

Where the operator is only a processor for a laboratory, the request should go to that laboratory first. The operator will assist the laboratory to the extent the application allows and the Terms require.

Security, children, and changes

The operator applies measures appropriate to a static marketing site and, for the application, measures appropriate to a business system that is not designed to hold patient results. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and the operator does not promise that it is.

The website and the application are directed at professionals. They are not directed at children, and the operator does not knowingly collect children’s data.

This notice may be updated by posting a new date on this page. The current text is the one published here. A change that materially affects a paid order is also handled as the Terms describe.

Contact

Privacy questions go to sales@auralab.ro, or through the contact form on the English homepage. Please describe the organisation and the request, and do not include patient data.
